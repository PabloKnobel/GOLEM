**Finding.** Each 1 °C higher 3-month mean temperature was associated with a 0.626% relative decrease in HbA1c (95% CI −0.977 to −0.274) in children, adjusting for PM2.5 and NO2 ([[wu2025air]], p. 3).

## Context
- Mexico City has mild temperatures with low variability; mean 15.67 °C (SD 1.97) (p. 2–3).
- Stronger in girls (−0.829%; −1.246 to −0.410) than boys (−0.480%; −1.050 to 0.095) (p. 3).
- Authors note the literature is mixed, with some studies linking heat and others cold to worse glycemia (p. 5).
