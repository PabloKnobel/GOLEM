**Finding.** An IQR increase in annual metal-industry PM2.5 was associated with 1.31% higher blood glucose (0.80–1.82%), robust to inverse probability weighting for missing glucose (1.25%; 0.75–1.76%) ([[krasnov2026association]], p. 501, Table 3).

## Context
- About 23% of responders lacked glucose results; IPW used to address selection (p. 499).
- Total PM2.5 mass (annual) was not associated: −0.45% (−0.99 to 0.09) (p. 501).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Apparent difference from [[pm25--glucose--knobel2024association]] (+0.78 mg/dL per IQR 6-month PM2.5 mass in the same cohort): the authors attribute it to the long-term vs 6-month exposure window (p. 500). Not filed as a contradiction because exposure windows differ.
