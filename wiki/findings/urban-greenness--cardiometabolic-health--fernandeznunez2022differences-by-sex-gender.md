**Finding.** Across 22 studies, urban greenness benefits were more often larger in females/women for obesity and other risk factors (63.6% of obesity studies) and in males/men for cardiovascular disease and CVD mortality ([[fernandeznunez2022differences]], p. 1057, 1060–1061).

## Context
- Vote counts, not pooled estimates; more than 90% of studies used sex and gender terms interchangeably (p. 1057).
- Counts in the text and Table 1 do not always agree (e.g., stroke, IHD, hypertension, children's obesity); see the `[!uncertain]` callout on [[fernandeznunez2022differences]].
- No included study assessed greenness quality (p. 1057).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Parallels the mental-health review by the same team, where 7 of 16 studies favored women ([[urban-greenness--mental-health--fernandeznunez2022gender-by-sex-gender]]).
