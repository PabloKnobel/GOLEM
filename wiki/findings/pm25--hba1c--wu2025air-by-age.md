**Finding.** The PM2.5–HbA1c association increased with age, from 0.132% at 4–5 years (CI crossing null) to 0.577% at 8–11 years (95% CI 0.289 to 0.866) ([[wu2025air]], p. 3).

## Context
- Authors suggest greater HbA1c variability with age, changing activity patterns, or cumulative exposure (p. 3–4).
- Visits at 8–11 years were more often in the warm season, with lower pollutant exposure (p. 3).
