**Finding.** All three low-SES indicators were associated with higher total PM2.5 and higher Soil and Crustal Dust, Heavy Fuel Oil and Industrial, Metal Processing Industry and Agricultural, and Motor Vehicle PM2.5, and with lower Coal and Oil Combustion and Biomass Burning PM2.5 ([[knobel2023socioeconomic]], p. 3).

> [!todo] Effect sizes are only shown graphically (Fig. 3–4) and in Supplementary Tables 2–3; not available in the converted text.

## Context
- Strength differed by indicator: % renters smallest for dust, strongest for Heavy Fuel Oil (p. 5).
- Dust–SES associations largest in the West, then South (p. 3, 7).
- Poverty reversed direction for three sources when all predictors were modeled jointly (p. 3).
