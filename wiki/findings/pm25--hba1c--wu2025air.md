**Finding.** Each 1 µg/m³ increase in 3-month PM2.5 was associated with a 0.311% relative increase in HbA1c (95% CI 0.159 to 0.464) in children, adjusting for NO2 and temperature ([[wu2025air]], p. 3).

## Context
- Adjusted for child age, season, maternal smoking, marital status and education, and a spline of year (p. 2).
- Single-exposure 0.264% (0.135 to 0.393); IPW 0.303% (0.151 to 0.457) (Table 2, p. 4).
- E-value 2.58 for PM2.5 (p. 3).
