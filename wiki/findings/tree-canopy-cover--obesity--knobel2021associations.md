**Finding.** Tracts in the highest tertile of tree canopy cover had 0.89 percentage points lower adult obesity prevalence than the lowest tertile (95% CI −2.05 to −0.28) ([[knobel2021associations]], p. 4).

## Context
- Adjusted for tract poverty, % non-Hispanic Black and population density (p. 3).
- CIs crossed the null for high blood pressure (−0.79; −1.95 to 0.37) and diabetes (−0.57; −1.24 to 0.1) (p. 4).
- Pattern restricted to low-poverty (−2.39; −4.26 to −0.52) and low % non-Hispanic Black tracts (−2.38; −4.14 to −0.62), without statistical evidence of modification by AIC (p. 4).
- Vegetation cover and NDVI were close to null in the same models (p. 4).
