**Finding.** The bird biodiversity dimension had the largest association with green space use (ERC 1.11; 1.04–1.19 per IQR) and was also associated with physical activity (OR 1.15; 1.01–1.31) and overweight/obesity (OR 0.88; 0.78–0.99) ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 6, Table 3).

## Context
- Adjusted for age, sex and education (p. 3). Text CIs differ slightly from Table 3; see paper page.
- Authors suggest a bioindicator role: bird diversity relates to plant biodiversity and soundscapes (p. 6).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Residential bird diversity was later associated with lower frailty in older New Yorkers ([[bird-biodiversity--frailty-index--knobel2026residential]]).
