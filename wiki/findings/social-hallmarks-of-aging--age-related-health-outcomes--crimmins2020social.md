**Finding.** In a model with both sets of measures, social variables explained 1.8 times the variance in 2-year mortality explained by biological aging measures, 36% more for multimorbidity, 4.7 times for cognitive functioning and 6.1 times for physical functioning ([[crimmins2020social]], p. 7).

## Context
- Biological measures: biological age minus chronological age (10 clinical markers), Horvath epigenetic age acceleration, telomere length, mtDNA copy number; age and sex included (p. 5–7).
- Direction "harmful": more social adversity predicted worse outcomes in the social-only model (p. 7).
- Variance shares, not causal effects; strong selective missingness (4,018 → 1,159) and short mortality window (p. 5, 7). Confidence medium for these reasons.
