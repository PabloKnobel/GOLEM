**Finding.** With age and sex controlled, the biological measures explained 9% of the variance in mortality, 8% in cognitive dysfunction, 10% in number of diseases and 6% in physical functioning; biological minus chronological age was strongly linked to all outcomes, epigenetic age acceleration to physical functioning and diseases, and telomere length and mtDNA copy number were not significant ([[crimmins2020social]], p. 7).

## Context
- When social variables were added, biological age − chronological age lost some explanatory power (p. 7).
- Same limitations as the companion finding: selective missingness, two-year mortality (p. 5, 7).
