**Finding.** An IQR higher mean overall quality of green spaces within 300 m was associated with higher odds of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity (OR 1.13; 1.00–1.27) in Barcelona adults ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 6, Table 3).

## Context
- Adjusted for age, sex and education (p. 3). Results text prints the CI as 1.0–1.31 (p. 4); see paper page.
- Strongest dimensions: surroundings and absence of incivilities (OR 1.19 each) (p. 6, Table 3).
- Green space use mediated 9.3% (0.8–47%; p = 0.042) (p. 5, Table 4).
- Lost significance with added covariates, excluding recent movers, or a 500 m buffer (p. 5).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Physical activity evidence for green space is inconsistent, possibly because quality is rarely measured ([[nieuwenhuijsen2017fifty]], p. 67, 69); this study measures quality directly.
