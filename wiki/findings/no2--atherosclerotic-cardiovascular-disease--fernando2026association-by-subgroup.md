**Finding.** The NO2–ischemic stroke/TIA association differed by urbanicity (LRT p = 0.030), sex (p = 0.009) and age (p = 0.035): cumulative ORs were similar for men and women and somewhat larger in urban residents, while women, adults <55 y and rural/suburban residents showed steeper lag-specific rises at lags 3–5 ([[fernando2026association]], p. 5–6, Table 3).

## Context
- Cumulative ORs: men 1.057 (1.025–1.089), women 1.058 (1.025–1.094); ≥55 y 1.058 (1.034–1.083), <55 y 1.044 (0.973–1.121) (Table 3).
- Lag 5: urban 1.025 (0.998–1.053) vs rural/suburban 1.066 (1.036–1.097) (p. 5).
- Diabetes (LRT p = 0.172), hypertension (0.875) and IHD (0.919) did not modify the association (Table 3).
- The LRT tests the shape/timing of the lag curve, not the cumulative OR (p. 4).
