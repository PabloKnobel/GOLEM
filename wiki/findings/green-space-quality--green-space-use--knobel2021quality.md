**Finding.** An IQR higher mean overall green space quality within 300 m was associated with 8% more days per week spent in green spaces (ERC 1.08; 1.01–1.15) ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 6, Table 3).

## Context
- Use = self-reported days per week of free time in parks, gardens or other green spaces (p. 3).
- Dimensions associated with use: access, aesthetics, safety, potential use, bird biodiversity (p. 5).
- Robust in all sensitivity analyses (p. 5).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Only one study in a 22-study review assessed greenness use ([[fernandeznunez2022differences]], p. 1057).
