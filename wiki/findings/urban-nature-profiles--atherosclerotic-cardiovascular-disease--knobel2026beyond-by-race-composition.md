**Finding.** The Street Trees profile was more protective against ASCVD in census tracts with higher percentages of non-Hispanic Black residents (interaction p = 0.02) ([[knobel2026beyond]], p. 4).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Contrasts with Philadelphia, where tree canopy looked protective mainly in low % non-Hispanic Black tracts, without statistical evidence of modification ([[knobel2021associations]], p. 4, 7). Different exposures (street-tree profile vs canopy) and outcomes.
