**Finding.** An IQR increase in annual metal-industry PM2.5 was associated with 8.35% higher diabetes risk (1.39–15.79%) among WTC general responders, mutually adjusted for four other sources ([[krasnov2026association]], p. 501, Table 3).

## Context
- Adjusted for age, sex, race, education, 9/11 occupation, time on site, tract SES, temperature and year (p. 499).
- Total PM2.5 mass: −6.66% (−16.33 to 4.12), null (p. 501, Table 3).
- Results text prints the lower CI as 1.49% (p. 499); see paper page.

## How it relates to other evidence
- Same cohort as [[knobel2024association]], which studied short- to medium-term PM2.5 mass and glucose.
