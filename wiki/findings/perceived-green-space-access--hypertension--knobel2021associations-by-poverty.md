**Finding.** The protective association between perceived park access and high blood pressure prevalence was seen in high- and medium-poverty tracts but not in low-poverty tracts ([[knobel2021associations]], p. 4).

## Context
- Little statistical evidence of effect modification (delta AIC −0.919); stratified estimates imprecise and the study underpowered for interaction (p. 4, 7). Confidence low.
- Authors interpret this as consistent with the equigenic hypothesis (p. 7).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Mixed evidence on stronger benefits for low-SES groups is summarized secondhand in [[markevych2017exploring]] (p. 310).
