**Finding.** Across 13 effect sizes from six longitudinal studies, each 10 dB increase in long-term residential noise was associated with a 15% higher hazard of incident ADRD (HR 1.15, 95% CI 1.03–1.28) ([[hwang2025comprehensive]], p. 6–7).

## Context
- No significant interaction between noise source and dementia subtype (QM = 2.38, df = 6, p = 0.88) (p. 6).
- Leave-one-out estimates 0.98–1.29; no small-study effects (Egger p = 0.132) (p. 6–7).
- Residual confounding by SES and air pollution possible; adjustment was not uniform (p. 8–9).
