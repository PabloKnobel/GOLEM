**Finding.** Each IQR increase in residential bird Shannon diversity was associated with a 1.4% lower expected frailty deficit count (95% CI −2.1% to −0.8%) in a cohort of 20,388 older adults ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 8).

## Context
- Negative binomial GAM; adjusted for age, sex, race and ethnicity, insurance, tract poverty and tract % non-Hispanic Black (p. 7).
- Robust to inverse-variance weighting and a quasi-Poisson model (p. 8, Table 2).
- Preprint, not peer reviewed; confidence set to medium for that reason.
