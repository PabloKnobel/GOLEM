**Finding.** An IQR higher mean overall green space quality within 300 m was associated with lower odds of overweight/obesity (self-reported BMI ≥25; OR 0.88; 0.79–0.98) ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 6, Table 3).

## Context
- Adjusted for age, sex and education; lost significance with neighborhood illiteracy or marital status added (p. 5).
- Strongest dimension: surroundings (OR 0.87); also amenities, absence of incivilities, bird biodiversity (p. 5–6).
- Physical activity mediated 9.7% (−1.3–42%; p = 0.066), not significant (p. 5, Table 4).
- Stratified results suggested stronger protection in females and higher education, with no significant interaction (p. 5).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Obesity was the most-studied outcome in a sex/gender review, where greenness more often benefited females/women ([[fernandeznunez2022differences]], p. 1057).
