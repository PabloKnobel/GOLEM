**Finding.** Eight of nine trials reported improved physical activity after green prescription, but in five of them both arms received a green prescription and no between-group difference was found ([[adewuyi2023health]], p. 11).

## Context
- Only three of nine trials used objective measures (accelerometers, pedometers) (p. 11).
- Direction set to mixed: the improvements are mostly within-group changes, not differences against a no-prescription control (Claude's reading of p. 11).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Observational evidence for physical activity as a green space pathway is also inconsistent ([[nieuwenhuijsen2017fifty]], p. 67).
