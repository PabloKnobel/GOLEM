**Finding.** An IQR increase in 6-month average PM2.5 was associated with 1.67 mg/dL higher total cholesterol (95% CI 1.00, 2.35) among WTC responders ([[knobel2024association]], p. 90–91).

> [!uncertain] The abstract gives 0.67 mg/dL with the same CI (1.00, 2.35); Results and Table 2 give 1.67, used here (p. 87, 91).

## Context
- 3-month window also associated (+0.75; 0.26, 1.23); 1-week null (0.32; −0.05, 0.69) (p. 91, Table 2).
- Abnormal cholesterol (>200 mg/dL): OR 1.08 (1.04, 1.12) per IQR (p. 92, Table 4).
- Stronger without diabetes (+1.92; 1.21, 2.64) than with (−0.44; −2.47, 1.58), contrary to prior evidence; authors suggest confounding by lipid-lowering medication (p. 91, 93).
