**Finding.** Of 24 randomized trials assessing psychological health and well-being, 16 reported a benefit of green prescription and none a harm ([[adewuyi2023health]], p. 11).

## Context
- Vote counting, not a pooled effect; meta-analysis was judged infeasible (p. 2).
- By type: forest therapy 7 of 8, horticultural therapy 4 of 6, green exercise 4 of 7, park prescription 1 of 2, distraction therapy 0 of 1 (p. 11).
- In 2 of the 16 positive trials both arms received a green prescription (p. 11).
- Risk of bias: no trial low overall; outcomes mostly self-reported and unblinded (p. 12). Confidence set to low for these reasons.
