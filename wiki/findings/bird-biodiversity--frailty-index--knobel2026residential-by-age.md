**Finding.** The bird diversity–frailty association was strongest at ages 65–74 (−3.0%, 95% CI −3.9 to −2.1), attenuated at 75–84 (−0.8%, −1.9 to 0.3) and absent at ≥85 (+1.6%, −0.0 to 3.3) ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 8–9).

## Context
- Interaction of IQR-scaled Shannon diversity with age group in the primary model (p. 7).
- The authors' explanation: declining life-space mobility and multimorbidity-driven deficit accumulation at older ages; they note that a review of green space and older adults also found larger benefits in "younger seniors" (p. 10). That review is secondhand, not yet in the vault.
