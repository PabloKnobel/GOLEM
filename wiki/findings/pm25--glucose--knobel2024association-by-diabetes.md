**Finding.** The PM2.5–glucose association was much larger among responders with diabetes (+5.03 mg/dL; 95% CI 2.04, 8.02) than without (+0.16; −0.19, 0.52) ([[knobel2024association]], p. 91).

## Context
- WTC exposure–glucose and –cholesterol associations were also larger with diabetes (p. 91, Fig. 1).
- Exception: PM2.5–cholesterol stronger without diabetes (p. 91).
- Authors cite inflammatory, coagulation and insulin-resistance mechanisms for diabetic susceptibility (p. 93).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Authors report agreement with Yitshak-Sade 2016 and Zanobetti 2002 on greater susceptibility with diabetes (secondhand via [[knobel2024association]], p. 93).
