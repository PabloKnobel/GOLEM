**Finding.** Among WTC Health Program responders, an IQR increase in 6-month average residential PM2.5 was associated with 0.78 mg/dL higher serum glucose (95% CI 0.30, 1.26), adjusting for WTC exposure ([[knobel2024association]], p. 90–91).

## Context
- Adjusted for WTC exposure category, sex, age, race, education, diabetes, hypertension, season, a year spline, matching-window temperature, plus a participant random intercept (p. 89).
- 1-week (−0.07; −0.33, 0.20) and 3-month (0.11; −0.24, 0.45) windows were null (p. 91, Table 2).
- Much larger among people with diabetes: [[pm25--glucose--knobel2024association-by-diabetes]].
- Table 2 header says "% Change" though text gives mg/dL (see source page).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Same direction as PM2.5 and child HbA1c in Mexico City: [[pm25--hba1c--wu2025air]].
