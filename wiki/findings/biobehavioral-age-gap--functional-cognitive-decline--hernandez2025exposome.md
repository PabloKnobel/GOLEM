**Finding.** Older adults with accelerated biobehavioral aging at wave 1 were more likely to show functional decline (RR 1.40, 95% CI 1.36–1.44) and cognitive decline (RR 1.25, 1.22–1.29) at wave 2. Country random-effects meta-analysis gave RR 3.57 (2.74–4.67) and 2.03 (1.75–2.36), with high heterogeneity ([[hernandez2025exposome]], p. 3092).

## Context
- BBAG is built partly from cognition and functional ability, so the outcomes overlap with the predictor. Re-estimating BBAG without the outcome variable gave "similar results with slightly smaller effects" (p. 3092, Extended Data Fig. 4).
- Wave-1 BBAG was divided by the time between waves (Methods). Only two waves were available (p. 3097).
