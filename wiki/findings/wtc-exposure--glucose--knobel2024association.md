**Finding.** High (vs low) WTC-related exposure in 2001 was associated with 0.82 mg/dL higher serum glucose (95% CI 0.22, 1.43) in 2004–2019, independent of later 6-month PM2.5 ([[knobel2024association]], p. 91).

## Context
- Intermediate vs low: glucose +0.36 (−0.16, 0.88); cholesterol +0.99 (0.30, 1.67), while high vs low cholesterol was +0.06 (−0.74, 0.86) (p. 91, Table 3).
- Dichotomized glucose >100 mg/dL: OR 1.08 (1.02, 1.14) for high exposure, though the text says significance was not maintained (p. 91–92, Table 4).
- Pre-2001 diabetes and hypertension were not associated with WTC exposure; PM2.5 mediation negligible (p. 91–92).
- No unexposed reference population; index self-reported and unvalidated (p. 93).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Authors note prior WTC studies are mixed on diabetes and metabolic syndrome (secondhand via [[knobel2024association]], p. 88, 93).
