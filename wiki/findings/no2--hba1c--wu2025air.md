**Finding.** NO2 was not associated with HbA1c in children in the multi-exposure model (−0.010%, 95% CI −0.126 to 0.106) ([[wu2025air]], p. 1, Table 2).

## Context
- The results text gives the upper CI as 0.091 instead of 0.106 (p. 3); Table 2 and abstract used here.
- Single-exposure 0.061% (−0.036 to 0.158) (Table 2, p. 4).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Contrasts with the PM2.5 result in the same model: [[pm25--hba1c--wu2025air]].
