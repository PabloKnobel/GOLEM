**Finding.** Census tracts in the highest tertile of perceived park or outdoor space access had 2.68 percentage points lower adult obesity prevalence than the lowest tertile (95% CI −3.93 to −1.43) ([[knobel2021associations]], p. 4).

## Context
- Adjusted for tract poverty, % non-Hispanic Black and population density (p. 3).
- Same direction for high blood pressure (−2.13; −3.35 to −0.9) and diabetes (−1.35; −2.06 to −0.64) (p. 4).
- Ecological design; reverse causation and self-selection possible (p. 7).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Stronger than the tree canopy association in the same tracts: [[tree-canopy-cover--obesity--knobel2021associations]].
- Effect modification by poverty: [[perceived-green-space-access--hypertension--knobel2021associations-by-poverty]].
