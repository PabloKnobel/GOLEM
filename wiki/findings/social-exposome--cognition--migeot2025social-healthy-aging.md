**Finding.** Among 1,175 healthy older controls, a more adverse multidimensional social exposome was associated with lower MMSE cognition (β 0.610, 95% CI 0.463–0.737), but not with functional ability (p = 0.101) or neuropsychiatric symptoms (p = 0.259) ([[migeot2025social]], p. 4).

## Context
- Lower model fit in this group: CFI 0.859, TLI 0.756, RMSEA 0.105, SRMR 0.092 (p. 4).
- Top indicators: assets 0.924, education 0.921, financial status 0.526, childhood labor 0.440, food insecurity 0.373 (p. 4).
- Authors attribute null functional/neuropsychiatric results to floor effects of those scales in healthy people (p. 9, 11).
- Reported CIs for the null outcomes are internally inconsistent (see [[migeot2025social]]).

## How it relates to other evidence
- Outcome is cognition in people without dementia, filed under [[dementia]] as the nearest outcome concept.
