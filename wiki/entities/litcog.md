**Lead.** A prospective cohort of 900 older adults in Chicago, led by Michael S. Wolf at Northwestern University (R01AG030611), used as the Aim 2 cohort of Pablo's urban exposome R01 ([[2026-grant-r01-urban-exposome-aging]], §B.4.1, §C.1).

> [!info] Infobox
> Enrolled Aug 2008–Oct 2010 from one academic general internal medicine clinic and six FQHCs; age 55–74 at entry, English-speaking, ≥2 clinic visits in the prior year · 900 at baseline; ~69% female; 46.7% Black, 46.5% White; mean baseline age 63.1 · In-person waves every 3 years, six waves over ~15 years · Measures: PROMIS (physical function, depression, anxiety), cognitive batteries incl. MMSE, LSNS-6, health literacy, chronic conditions ([[2026-grant-r01-urban-exposome-aging]], §B.4.1–B.4.2, Tables 4–5)

## Notes
- Attrition: roughly 55% lost by wave 4 ([[2026-grant-r01-urban-exposome-aging]], §B.4.4).
- Residential address histories available for tract-year exposure linkage ([[2026-grant-r01-urban-exposome-aging]], §B.2).
- Baseline mean PM2.5 12.0 (SD 0.85) and NDVI 0.27 (0.13) as reported in the grant (Table 4).
- Data access and analysis through Northwestern CAHRA (Wolf, Rogers) ([[2026-grant-r01-urban-exposome-aging]], §C.2).
