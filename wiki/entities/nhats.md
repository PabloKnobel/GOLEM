**Lead.** A nationally representative panel of US Medicare beneficiaries aged 65 and older, interviewed in person every year since 2011; the data source for Pablo's nature-and-impairment pilot ([[2026-grant-heals-nature-impairment-plan]], §4).

> [!info] Infobox
> 2011 cohort of 8,245; new samples 2015, 2022, 2023; oversamples the oldest old and Black beneficiaries · Measures vision, hearing, SPPB, dementia classification, going out, participation, death · Tract linkage and death dates need restricted data (DUA + IRB), analyzed in a secure enclave ([[2026-grant-heals-nature-impairment-plan]], §4)

## Notes
- Round 10 (2020) was conducted by phone and has no physical performance tests ([[2026-grant-heals-nature-impairment-plan]], §4).
- Enclave rule: no results below Census Division may leave the enclave ([[2026-grant-heals-nature-impairment-plan]], §4).
- Pablo's public-file panel: 13,712 community-dwelling participants in metropolitan counties, 57,262 person-rounds, 3,378 deaths ([[2026-grant-heals-nature-impairment-plan]], §4).
