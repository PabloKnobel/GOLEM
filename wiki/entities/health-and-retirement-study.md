**Lead.** A large nationally representative US study of older adults, begun in 1992, that added venous blood collection in 2016, enabling biological aging measures alongside detailed social data ([[crimmins2020social]], p. 5).

> [!info] Infobox
> **Coverage:** United States, older adults · **Start:** 1992 · **Biomarkers:** 2016 Venous Blood Study, subsample of 4,018 · **Documentation:** https://hrs.isr.umich.edu/documentation (as cited in [[crimmins2020social]], p. 5) · **Access:** ? (not stated in the vault's sources)

## Notes
- 2016 biomarkers used by Crimmins: Levine biological age (10 clinical markers), Horvath epigenetic clock, telomere length, mtDNA copy number; polygenic scores from HRS for non-Hispanic whites ([[crimmins2020social]], p. 5–7).
- Complete-case analysis kept 1,159 of 4,018; missingness was socially and health patterned ([[crimmins2020social]], p. 5).
