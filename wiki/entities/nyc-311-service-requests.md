**Lead.** New York City's open record of non-emergency resident complaints (noise, sanitation, housing, rodents and more), each with coordinates and a timestamp; Pablo's source for social and perceived stressor exposures ([[2025-grant-p30-311-frailty]], p. 3).

> [!info] Infobox
> Source: NYC Open Data · Years used: 2011–2023 (2025 pilot) · Resolution: address and day ([[2025-grant-p30-311-frailty]], p. 4; [[2026-loi-heals-311-cvd]])

## Notes
- Pablo frames complaints as mixing objective conditions, population sensitivity and propensity to complain ([[2025-grant-p30-311-frailty]], p. 4).
- His team had already produced overall yearly census-tract 311 rates by December 2025 ([[2025-grant-p30-311-frailty]], p. 4).
