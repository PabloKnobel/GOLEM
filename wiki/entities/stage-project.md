**Lead.** STAGE is a Horizon Europe consortium (2024–2029, coordinated by the University of Oulu) on healthy ageing and multi-morbidity across the life course, combining a cross-EU neighbourhood atlas, the [[exposome]], biological hallmarks of ageing and AI prediction (raw/web/stage-healthyageing-eu.md). ## Notes
- Overarching concept: "the risk of ageing with multi-morbidity is preventable and treatable" (website, home).
- Framing of aims: "researching the root causes of unhealthy ageing" through societal and historical barriers, a "healthy ageing index and atlas" of European environments, multi-morbidity trajectories "in cohorts and biobanks", "molecular hallmarks of ageing" and exposome–genome interaction (website, About).
- Data and tools: FAIR data, open-source AI to predict multi-morbidity trajectories, European health and environmental data catalogues linked to environmental and economic models for policy, a person-centred digital platform (website, About).
- Work packages: WP1 management; WP2 ethical, legal, social, historical and infrastructural preparedness; WP3 cross-EU healthy ageing neighbourhood atlas; WP4 moderators of ageing with multi-morbidity; WP5 biological hallmarks of ageing; WP6 AI predictive modelling; WP7 person-centred applications; WP8 life-course clinical studies; WP9 life-course and spatial data portal; WP10 engagement and impact (website, Work packages).
