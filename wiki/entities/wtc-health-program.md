**Lead.** A cohort of workers and volunteers who took part in rescue, recovery and clean-up after the September 11, 2001 World Trade Center attack, with about two decades of monitoring data; Pablo used it to study an extreme air pollution event alongside later PM2.5 ([[knobel2024association]], p. 88).

> [!info] Infobox
> **Coverage:** responders; excludes Pentagon, Shanksville and FDNY responders · **Years:** monitoring visits 2004–2019 in the vault's source · **Size:** 22,447 participants analyzed · **Access:** restricted by data use agreement with the WTC Health Program Data Center ([[knobel2024association]], p. 88, 94)

## Notes
- Over 90,000 people were involved in the rescue, recovery and clean-up effort ([[knobel2024association]], p. 88).
- Exposure questionnaire at first visit; interviewer-administered medical questionnaire, physical exam, labs and pulmonary function at every visit ([[knobel2024association]], p. 88).
- Address history collected only from 2012 ([[knobel2024association]], p. 89).
- 34,764 Tri-state general responders 2003–2019; annual medical questionnaire with self-reported physician diagnoses; labs from Mount Sinai, NYU, Stony Brook and Northwell centers ([[krasnov2026association]], p. 498).
