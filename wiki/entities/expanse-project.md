**Lead.** EXPANSE is a five-year Horizon 2020 consortium coordinated by Utrecht University on the urban [[exposome]] and cardio-metabolic and pulmonary disease, built around external and internal exposome work packages, interventions and a public toolbox (raw/web/expanseproject-eu.md). Pablo keeps it as a model of a large EU proposal: its language, style and content.

## Notes
- Aim, framed as a question for planners, policy makers and residents: "How to maximize one's health in a modern urban environment?" (website, Background).
- Tagline: "Exposome powered tools for healthy living in urban settings" (website, home).
- Work packages: WP1 External exposome (food, lifestyle, social, built, physico-chemical; Swiss TPH); WP2 Internal exposome ("biological responses to cumulative exposures", OMICS, ultra-high-resolution mass spectrometry; Helmholtz); WP3 Exposome–health, cardio-metabolic and pulmonary disease (CMPD; Karolinska); WP4 Interventions to prevent CMPD by modifying the urban exposome (ISGlobal); WP5 Methods (Imperial); WP6 Toolbox (Utrecht); WP7 Exposome Hub for stakeholders (Tartu) (website, Work packages).
- The site's partner list shows 12 European countries plus the USA, not the 14 stated.
- Claude's reading: EXPANSE PI names overlap with authors in the vault's exposome papers (e.g., a C. Delpierre co-authored [[neufcourt2022assessing]]); same person not confirmed.

- Exposure work package output: harmonised maps of 33 external exposome factors for Europe, 2000–2020, openly available via the Exposome maps platform. The health data span administrative, child and adult cohorts of "over 25 million individuals" ([[dehoogh2025europewide]], p. 2, 13).
- Full name: "EXposome Powered tools for healthy living in urbAN Settings" ([[dehoogh2025europewide]], p. 2).
