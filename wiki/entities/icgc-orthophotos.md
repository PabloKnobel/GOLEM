**Lead.** Annual 25 cm aerial orthophotos of all of Catalonia since 2012, released openly by the ICGC; the imagery for Aim 1 of Pablo's RyC proposal ([[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.2.2).

> [!info] Infobox
> Coverage: all of Catalonia, yearly, 25 cm, from 2012 · License: CC BY 4.0 · Access: ICGC download service or web map services ([[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.2.2)

## Notes
- Pablo's Girona pilot processed 87 census sections (~42,000 tiles, 2025) through the web map service ([[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.5).
- Transfer to the rest of Spain planned through the national PNOA program ([[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §2.1.3).
