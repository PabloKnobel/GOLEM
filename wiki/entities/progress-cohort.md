**Lead.** Programming Research in Obesity, Growth, Environment, and Social Stressors: a Mexico City pregnancy and birth cohort followed into childhood, used with Mount Sinai collaborators for environmental exposure studies ([[wu2025air]], p. 1–2).

> [!info] Infobox
> **Coverage:** Mexico City · **Recruitment:** 1,054 mothers in the second trimester; children born July 2007–February 2011 · **Source population:** IMSS (Mexican Social Security Institute) · **Access:** data not shareable per [[wu2025air]] (p. 6)

## Notes
- Eligibility: mothers over 18, under 20 weeks pregnant, free of heart or kidney disease, no steroid or anti-epilepsy drugs, no daily alcohol, phone access, planning to stay in Mexico City three years ([[wu2025air]], p. 2).
- Child HbA1c measured at 4–5, 6–7 and 8–11 years (2013–2019); geocoded residential histories available ([[wu2025air]], p. 2).
- IMSS serves over 55% of Mexico's population, per the authors ([[wu2025air]], p. 5).
