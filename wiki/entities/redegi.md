**Lead.** Population-based registry of incident dementia diagnosed in the public hospitals of the Girona Health Region since 2007; the source of severity at diagnosis for Aim 2b of Pablo's RyC proposal ([[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.2.4).

> [!info] Infobox
> ~700 new cases a year; MMSE at diagnosis for >95% of cases; run by the Institut d'Assistència Sanitària; access through a data use agreement ([[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.2.4)

## Notes
- Previously linked to SIDIAP with encoded identifiers in a study of 4,996 patients (Ponjoan et al., 2019b; secondhand via [[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.2.4).
- Pablo has contacted the ReDeGi and SIDIAP teams about access ([[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.2.4).
