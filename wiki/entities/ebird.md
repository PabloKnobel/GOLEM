**Lead.** A citizen-science bird observation platform run by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology, where mostly amateur birders submit checklists of the species seen or heard on each outing ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 4).

> [!info] Infobox
> **Coverage:** checklists with date, location, duration, distance · **Known biases:** clusters near roads, trails and wealthier, predominantly white neighborhoods · **Used in:** [[knobel2026residential]]

## Notes
- In NYC 2010–2024, only 7.2% of census tract-years had enough complete checklists to compute diversity directly ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 5).
- Complete checklists (all species recorded) are needed to compute encounter rates ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 5).
