**Lead.** Electronic health records from the Mount Sinai Health System in New York City, accessed through the Mount Sinai Data Warehouse; Pablo uses them for neighborhood-exposure studies of older adults ([[knobel2025social]], p. 242). Only summaries are filed here; no patient-level data.

> [!info] Infobox
> **Coverage:** NYC patients treated at MSHS · **Years in vault:** 2011–2022 ([[knobel2025social]]) · **Access:** IRB-approved, not shareable (p. 247)

## Notes
- Addresses updated at each encounter when a change is reported; SVI linked yearly by last address ([[knobel2025social]], p. 242).
- Inpatient and outpatient labs available; biomarkers for PhenoAge missing in 65.8% of person-years ([[knobel2025social]], p. 242, 246).
- Primary care patients ≥55 y, 2013–2022; ASCVD from ICD-10 codes; person-time censored at address change or two years without encounters ([[knobel2026beyond]], p. 2).
