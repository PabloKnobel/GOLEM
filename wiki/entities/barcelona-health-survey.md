**Lead.** A periodic (every five years) population survey of the health status, lifestyles and use of health services of Barcelona residents; its 2016 edition supplied the outcomes for Pablo's study linking [[recital]] green space quality to physical activity, overweight/obesity and green space use ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 2).

> [!info] Infobox
> **Coverage:** Barcelona, 10 districts · **Edition used:** 2016 · **Sample:** face-to-face home interviews of 4,000 people; 2,053 adults analyzed · **Sampling:** random from the municipal register, stratified by age and sex across districts ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 2)

## Notes
- Non-responders were replaced by randomly preselected people with the same age, sex and district ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 2).
- Pablo's analysis excluded people under 18, people with limitations to walk or leave home, and those without a green space within 300 m ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 2).
- Green space use item: days per week of free time spent in parks, gardens or other green spaces ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 3); see [[green-space-use]].
- Two-year gap between the 2016 survey and the 2018 RECITAL audits ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 7).
- The 2011 edition was used in an earlier Barcelona study (Dadvand et al., 2016; secondhand via [[knobel2021quality]], p. 6).
