**Lead.** Pseudonymized primary care records of the people assigned to the Catalan Institute of Health, the cohort for Aim 2a of Pablo's RyC proposal ([[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.2.3).

> [!info] Infobox
> About 5.8 million people, 328 primary care centers, ~75% of Catalonia · Diagnoses, prescriptions, measurements, residence history and census section; linkable to hospital discharge · Access via SIDIAP scientific committee + IDIAPJGol ethics committee ([[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.2.3)

## Notes
- Dementia diagnoses validated with positive predictive value 91% (Ponjoan et al., 2019a; secondhand via [[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.2.3).
- Already linked to census-section NO2 and PM2.5 (de Bont et al., 2021; secondhand via [[2026-grant-ryc-continuum-proposal]], §3.2.3).
- Also the data source of Pablo's separate planned SIDIAP project (not in the vault).
