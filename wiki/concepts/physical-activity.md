**Lead.** Bodily movement measured as intensity or volume, studied both as an outcome of green space and as a mediator to cardiometabolic health — an "instoration" (building capacities) pathway ([[markevych2017exploring]], p. 302–303). Evidence that green space raises physical activity is inconsistent, possibly because quality is rarely measured ([[nieuwenhuijsen2017fifty]], p. 67, 69). Promoted from candidates as primary outcome of [[knobel2021quality]].

> [!info] Infobox
> **Kind:** outcome / mediator · **Typical measures:** IPAQ-short (self-report), accelerometers, pedometers · **Key sources:** [[knobel2021quality]], [[adewuyi2023health]], [[nieuwenhuijsen2017fifty]]

## Definition and measurement
- IPAQ-short: walking, moderate and vigorous activity in the last seven days; dichotomized as inactive/low vs moderate-to-vigorous ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 3).
- Only three of nine green prescription RCTs measured physical activity objectively ([[adewuyi2023health]], p. 11).
- Pathway-matched measurement: physical activity requires green space perceived as usable, accessible, inclusive or safe ([[knobel2021associations]], p. 2).

## Evidence
- Higher audited green space quality within 300 m was associated with moderate-to-vigorous activity (OR 1.13; 1.00–1.27); surroundings and absence of incivilities were the strongest dimensions ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 6, Table 3) → [[green-space-quality--physical-activity--knobel2021quality]].
- Green space use mediated 9.3% of the quality–activity association; physical activity mediated 9.7% of quality–overweight/obesity, not significant ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 5, Table 4).
- Green prescription: 8 of 9 RCTs reported improved physical activity, mostly within-group ([[adewuyi2023health]], p. 11) → [[green-prescription--physical-activity--adewuyi2023health]].
- Physical activity mediation is mixed across studies (secondhand via [[markevych2017exploring]], p. 305).
- In greenness–obesity studies, physical activity is proposed as the mediator, and gendered use patterns (men in sports fields, women on walking trails) are discussed (secondhand via [[fernandeznunez2022differences]], p. 1057, 1062).
