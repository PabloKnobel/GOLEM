**Lead.** The social ties and contacts that green spaces may foster, proposed as an "instoration" (building capacities) pathway alongside physical activity ([[markevych2017exploring]], p. 302–303). Promoted from candidates at 5 mentions; in the vault it appears only as a proposed mechanism, with no primary estimate yet.

> [!info] Infobox
> **Kind:** mechanism · **Also known as:** social contacts · **Key sources:** [[markevych2017exploring]], [[nieuwenhuijsen2017fifty]]

## Definition and measurement
- One of the four conventional pathways (air quality, physical activity, social contacts, stress), grouped under building capacities in the three-domain model ([[markevych2017exploring]], p. 302–303).

## Evidence
- Social cohesion may mediate mental and general health ([[markevych2017exploring]], p. 305).
- One mediation study estimated stress and social cohesion at ~20–40% of the green space–health association (secondhand via [[nieuwenhuijsen2017fifty]], p. 68).
- Listed among mechanisms motivating exposure selection for urban nature profiles; Park Access is read as acting partly through social interaction ([[knobel2026beyond]], p. 4).
- Cited as a mechanism in a Barcelona green space quality study, not measured there ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 2).
