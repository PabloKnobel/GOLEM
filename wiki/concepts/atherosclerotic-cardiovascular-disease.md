**Lead.** Clinical cardiovascular disease caused by atherosclerosis — myocardial infarction, chronic ischemic heart disease, ischemic stroke, peripheral arterial disease and aortic atherosclerosis — as defined for primary prevention by the 2019 ACC/AHA guideline ([[knobel2026beyond]], p. 2). It is the hard-endpoint counterpart of the risk factors on [[cardiometabolic-health]].

> [!info] Infobox
> **Kind:** outcome · **Also known as:** ASCVD; broader CVD in reviews · **Typical measures:** ICD-10 codes in EHR (I21, I25.1/.7/.8, I63–I66, I70.0, I70.2, I73.9); CVD mortality · **Key sources:** [[knobel2026beyond]], [[fernandeznunez2022differences]]

## Definition and measurement
- EHR definition: earliest ICD-10 match for acute MI, chronic ischemic coronary heart disease, ischemic stroke, peripheral arterial disease or aortic atherosclerosis; prevalent cases before baseline excluded ([[knobel2026beyond]], p. 2, Table S1).
- Reviews of greenness also group stroke, ischemic heart disease, CVD and CVD mortality ([[fernandeznunez2022differences]], p. 1058–1061).
- Diabetes raises CVD risk (coronary artery disease, heart failure, stroke) two- to fourfold (secondhand via [[krasnov2026association]], p. 497).

## Evidence
- In 36,830 older New Yorkers, Waterfront, Park Access and Street Trees profiles had lower ASCVD risk than Low green (HR 0.88–0.92); High Cover was not clearly protective ([[knobel2026beyond]], p. 4) → [[urban-nature-profiles--atherosclerotic-cardiovascular-disease--knobel2026beyond]].
- Park Access more protective in males; Street Trees more protective in tracts with more non-Hispanic Black residents ([[knobel2026beyond]], p. 4) → [[urban-nature-profiles--atherosclerotic-cardiovascular-disease--knobel2026beyond-by-sex]], [[urban-nature-profiles--atherosclerotic-cardiovascular-disease--knobel2026beyond-by-race-composition]].
- A review found greenness benefits for CVD (2 of 2 studies) and most CVD-mortality studies larger in males/men ([[fernandeznunez2022differences]], p. 1060–1061) → [[urban-greenness--cardiometabolic-health--fernandeznunez2022differences-by-sex-gender]].
- The same review notes possible late or wrong CVD diagnosis in women as an alternative explanation (secondhand, [[fernandeznunez2022differences]], p. 1063).
- Short-term NO2 was associated with ischemic stroke or TIA admissions in a nationwide Israeli case-crossover study (OR 1.057 per 8 ppb, lag 0–5) ([[fernando2026association]], p. 4) → [[no2--atherosclerotic-cardiovascular-disease--fernando2026association]]. Outcome here is ICD-9 ischemic stroke/TIA from a stroke registry (p. 3).
