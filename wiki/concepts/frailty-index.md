**Lead.** Frailty is cumulative decline across physiological systems that raises vulnerability to adverse events. Frailty indices measure it as the number or proportion of accumulated health deficits ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 2, 6).

> [!info] Infobox
> **Kind:** outcome / measure · **Variant in the vault:** VA-FI, 31 items from ICD-10 and CPT codes · **Key sources:** [[knobel2026residential]]

## Definition and measurement
- VA-FI items follow the Searle et al. criteria: linked to health status, more common with age, not saturated before 65, covering several systems ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 6).
- Applied annually with a 3-year code lookback; analyzed as a deficit count rather than a proportion ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 6–7).
- EHR-based indices also reflect how often patients use care, a source of bias ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 11).
