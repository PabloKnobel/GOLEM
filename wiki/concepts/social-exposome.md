**Lead.** The social exposome is the entirety of a person's social environment treated as part of the exposome, alongside the physical environment. The main framework in the vault adds an equity and environmental justice lens and explains how social exposures become health outcomes ([[gudimindermann2023integrating]], p. 1).

> [!info] Infobox
> **Kind:** conceptual framework · **Core principles:** multidimensionality, reciprocity, timing and continuity · **Transmission pathways:** embodiment; resilience and susceptibility or vulnerability; empowerment · **Key sources:** [[gudimindermann2023integrating]]

## Definition and measurement
- Dimensions: social interactions (actors, relational dynamics, places) embedded in socioeconomic and sociodemographic circumstances; systems, institutions and priorities of the political economy; social norms; cultural values; all shaped by social inequalities and structural injustices, with intersectionality and gender ([[gudimindermann2023integrating]], p. 7–10, Fig. 1).
- Places of social interaction include the neighborhood physical environment, including green and blue spaces ([[gudimindermann2023integrating]], p. 9, Table 2).
- Operationalization is proposed dimension by dimension, combining individual scales with area-level administrative statistics; the authors acknowledge it may be too generic ([[gudimindermann2023integrating]], p. 15–16).
- Earlier related labels: socio-exposome, public health exposome, psychoexposome, compound exposome (secondhand via [[gudimindermann2023integrating]], p. 2).
- Ibanez et al. define the social exposome as "aggregate- and individual-level factors related to disparity and inequality", with Gini and HDI as aggregate measures ([[ibanez2024neuroecological]], p. 4).

## Evidence
- Conceptual only so far; empirical validation was planned in the Equal-Life project ([[gudimindermann2023integrating]], p. 16).
- Exposure differentials (socially patterned exposure) and differential vulnerability (social effect modification) are kept as distinct mechanisms ([[gudimindermann2023integrating]], p. 14).
- First empirical use in the vault: the four CDC SVI themes were modeled as a mixture "from a social exposome perspective" for biological aging in older New Yorkers ([[knobel2025social]], p. 241, 243) → [[social-vulnerability--phenotypic-age-acceleration--knobel2025social]].
- Individual-level multidimensional social exposome (MSE) across the lifespan (ten dimensions, 319 items) was associated with cognition, functional ability and neuropsychiatric symptoms in 2,211 Latin American HC, AD and FTLD participants; the combined score outperformed single dimensions ([[migeot2025social]], p. 3–6) → [[social-exposome--dementia--migeot2025social]], [[social-exposome--cognition--migeot2025social-healthy-aging]].
- A scoping review found 73 empirical exposome studies using social variables to Jan 2022; most used them as exposures or confounders, and only 8 of 35 hypothesis-driven studies discussed a social mechanism ([[neufcourt2022assessing]], p. 3–4). Consistent with the argument that social variables are often treated only as confounders ([[gudimindermann2023integrating]], p. 14).
