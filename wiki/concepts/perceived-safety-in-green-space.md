**Lead.** Safety and incivilities (signs of disorder or misuse) are two of the quality dimensions used to audit urban green spaces; crime and fear of crime are listed among the adverse pathways that can offset green space health benefits ([[knobel2019systematic]], p. 2–3; [[markevych2017exploring]], p. 303).

> [!info] Infobox
> **Kind:** exposure (quality dimension) · **Also known as:** incivilities, fear of crime · **Instruments:** [[recital]] · **Key sources:** [[knobel2019systematic]], [[knobel2021quality]]

## Definition and measurement
- Among 15 green space quality tools, incivilities were covered by 11 and safety by 8 ([[knobel2019systematic]], p. 5, Table 4).
- [[recital]] has separate Incivilities and Safety dimensions (Cronbach's alpha 0.71 and 0.7); incivilities had the lowest inter-rater reliability, attributed to timing (noise, smells) and subjectivity ([[knobel2021development]], p. 4–5).

## Evidence
- In Barcelona, absence of incivilities was among the strongest quality dimensions for moderate-to-vigorous physical activity (OR 1.19) and was associated with lower overweight/obesity; safety was associated with green space use ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 4–6, Table 3) → [[green-space-quality--physical-activity--knobel2021quality]].
