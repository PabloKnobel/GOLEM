**Lead.** The variety of bird species in a place, used in health research as an exposure that captures nature *quality* rather than amount. It acts both as a direct experiential exposure (sight, song) and as a bioindicator of wider environmental quality ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 3).

> [!info] Infobox
> **Kind:** exposure · **Typical measures:** species richness; Shannon diversity (richness + evenness) · **Main data source:** [[ebird]] citizen science · **Key sources:** [[knobel2026residential]]

## Definition and measurement
- Shannon diversity computed from species encounter rates on complete eBird checklists, then modeled as a latent tract × year surface with a Gamma GAM to correct uneven observer effort ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 4–6).
- Raw eBird data cluster near roads, trails and wealthier, predominantly white neighborhoods, so raw counts give biased exposure estimates ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 4).
- Coarse units (ZIP code, county) mostly reflect general environmental quality rather than what people encounter near home ([[knobel2026residential]], p. 3).
- Species richness and abundance, and species and land cover diversity, were proposed as expanded greenspace metrics adapted from urban ecosystem service studies ([[markevych2017exploring]], p. 309).

## Evidence
- Higher residential bird Shannon diversity was associated with lower frailty severity in older adults: −1.4% expected deficit count per IQR (95% CI −2.1 to −0.8) → [[bird-biodiversity--frailty-index--knobel2026residential]].
- The association was strongest at ages 65–74 → [[bird-biodiversity--frailty-index--knobel2026residential-by-age]].
- Studies cited by [[knobel2026residential]] (p. 2, 9) link bird richness or diversity to life expectancy, life satisfaction and self-rated mental health (Chen 2023; Methorst 2021; Buxton 2024). Secondhand; not yet in the vault.
- Distinct from the "biodiversity hypothesis" of microbiota and immune function ([[nieuwenhuijsen2017fifty]], p. 67).
- Animal biodiversity was included in only 3 of 15 multi-dimensional green space quality audit tools reviewed in 2019 ([[knobel2019systematic]], p. 5, Table 4).
- RECITAL includes a bird biodiversity dimension scored by order presence; in Barcelona its alpha was 0.4 (0.17, 0.58) and item ICCs ranged 0.51–0.94, with three orders lacking variability for ICC ([[knobel2021development]], p. 4, 6).
