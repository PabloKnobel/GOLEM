**Lead.** Outcomes describing cardiovascular and metabolic risk: obesity, high blood pressure, diabetes and glycemic markers such as HbA1c. Obesity, high blood pressure and diabetes are among the most important cardiovascular disease risk factors ([[knobel2021associations]], p. 1).

> [!info] Infobox
> **Kind:** outcome (grouped) · **Measures in vault:** tract % obese, % ever-diagnosed high blood pressure and diabetes; child HbA1c; trial blood pressure, BMI, weight · **Key sources:** [[knobel2021associations]], [[wu2025air]], [[adewuyi2023health]]

## Definition and measurement
- HbA1c reflects average blood glucose over the preceding two to three months and is not affected by postprandial fluctuation ([[wu2025air]], p. 1).

## Evidence
- Green space: perceived park access and tree canopy were inversely associated with tract obesity, high blood pressure and diabetes in Philadelphia; NDVI was null ([[knobel2021associations]], p. 4) → [[perceived-green-space-access--obesity--knobel2021associations]], [[tree-canopy-cover--obesity--knobel2021associations]].
- Green prescription: 5 of 9 RCTs reported cardiometabolic benefits ([[adewuyi2023health]], p. 11).
- Air pollution and temperature: PM2.5 associated with higher and temperature with lower HbA1c in children ([[wu2025air]], p. 3) → [[pm25--hba1c--wu2025air]], [[ambient-temperature--hba1c--wu2025air]].
- Air pollution in adults: 6-month PM2.5 associated with higher serum glucose and total cholesterol, and high WTC disaster exposure with higher glucose, in WTC responders ([[knobel2024association]], p. 90–91) → [[pm25--glucose--knobel2024association]], [[pm25--total-cholesterol--knobel2024association]], [[wtc-exposure--glucose--knobel2024association]].
- Green space quality: higher audited quality of green spaces within 300 m was associated with lower odds of overweight/obesity (OR 0.88; 0.79–0.98) in Barcelona adults ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 6) → [[green-space-quality--overweight-obesity--knobel2021quality]].
- Sex/gender: in a review of 22 studies, greenness benefits for obesity were more often larger in females/women (63.6% of obesity studies) ([[fernandeznunez2022differences]], p. 1057) → [[urban-greenness--cardiometabolic-health--fernandeznunez2022differences-by-sex-gender]].
- Source-apportioned PM2.5: metal-industry PM2.5 was associated with diabetes (+8.35%) and glucose (+1.31%) in WTC responders, while total annual PM2.5 mass was null ([[krasnov2026association]], p. 501) → [[metal-industry-pm25--diabetes--krasnov2026association]], [[metal-industry-pm25--glucose--krasnov2026association]].
- Clinical endpoints (MI, ischemic stroke, PAD) are filed under [[atherosclerotic-cardiovascular-disease]] ([[knobel2026beyond]], p. 2).
