**Lead.** One Health is an approach that "recognizes the inseparable connections between human, animal, and environmental health" and is used for zoonoses, food safety, antimicrobial resistance, contamination and climate impacts ([[ibanez2024neuroecological]], p. 3). In the vault it appears as the ecological counterpart of the [[exposome]] in a brain-health framing.

> [!info] Infobox
> **Kind:** theory / approach · **Also known as:** One Health approach · **Key sources:** [[ibanez2024neuroecological]]

## Definition and measurement
- Human, animal and environmental health treated as one system; multidisciplinary by design ([[ibanez2024neuroecological]], p. 3).
- Ibanez et al. place the exposome as input to One Health and "green skills" (part of brain capital) as the human capacity to act on it ([[ibanez2024neuroecological]], p. 5, Fig. 1).
