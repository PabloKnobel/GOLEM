**TL;DR.** RECITAL is an in-situ green space quality audit with 90 items in 11 dimensions, built for health research; applied to 149 Barcelona green spaces, mean item ICC was 0.84 (35% excellent, 45% good, 19% moderate, one poor), and dimension Cronbach's alpha ranged from −0.2 to 0.8 ([[knobel2021development]], p. 1, 4–6). Pablo is first author (corresponding: Dadvand).

## Key points
- Built from BRAT-DO, NEST and POST with a cross-disciplinary expert team; dimensions chosen from the 2019 tools review ([[knobel2019systematic]]) (p. 2).
- Dimensions: Surroundings, Access, Facilities, Amenities, Aesthetics and Attractions, Incivilities, Safety, Potential usage, Land Covers, Animal biodiversity, Birds biodiversity (p. 2, Table 1).
- Scoring: 0–4 scale with six methods (quantity, reversed quantity, quality, combined, potential use, Braun-Blanquet cover classes) (p. 2, 4, Table 2).
- Data can be used as single items, dimension scores or an overall score (p. 2).
- UGS definition: six criteria (in urban municipality area, vegetated, own identity, connected, public access, mainly recreational); polygons <20 m apart merged; >1 ha only (p. 3).
- Training: four-day workshop including one day of species recognition; eight fieldworkers, 10 AM–2 PM, no rain (p. 3).
- Five biodiversity items had no ICC due to no variability (Chiroptera, Falconiformes/Accipitriformes, Coraciiformes, Strigiformes, Blattodea) (p. 4).
- Highest reliability in Land Covers and Facilities; lowest in Incivilities, attributed to timing (noise, smells) and subjectivity (p. 4–5).
- Dimensions with alpha <0.7 interpreted as causal (formative) indicators; negative alpha suggests mutually exclusive items (p. 6).
- Mean UGS size 2.4 ha; largest 27.6 ha (p. 4).
- Authors' limitations: 1-ha minimum for practical reasons; biodiversity assessment hard for non-experts; tailored to Barcelona (p. 7).
