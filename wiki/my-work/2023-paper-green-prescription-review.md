## How I framed it
- Rationale for an RCT-only review: green prescription "relies on the active involvement of clinicians", and persuading them "can be challenging, especially when evidence is derived from various epidemiological study designs" (p. 2).
- Two routes to nature contact: "bringing nature to people" (greening) vs "bringing people to nature" (green prescription) (p. 1).
- Closing message: "Green prescriptions are complex interventions that surpass simple advice or recommendations" (p. 14).
