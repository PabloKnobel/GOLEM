## How I framed it
- Gap: the only prior meta-analysis included MCI and occupational noise, which "complicates the interpretation and generalizability" for environmental noise (p. 2).
- Exposome call: "Future research should include the exposome approach for identifying environmental drivers of dementia" (abstract, p. 1).
- "By enabling simultaneous consideration of multiple exposures, exposomics offers a more holistic and policy-relevant strategy for identifying environmental drivers of dementia" (p. 8).
