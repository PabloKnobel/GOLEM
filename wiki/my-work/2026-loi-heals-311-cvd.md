**Lead.** Letter of intent (due October 8, 2026) for "Everyday Urban Stressors from NYC 311 Complaints and Chronic and Acute Cardiovascular Disease", a resubmission of the 2025 311 pilot ([[2025-grant-p30-311-frailty]]) with the outcome switched from frailty to cardiovascular disease. Latest version of the 311 project (per Pablo, 2026-11-16). Hub: [[nyc-311-exposures]].

> [!info] LOI not approved (Pablo, 2026-11-16).

## How I framed it
- 311 records are "a free by-product of city management" giving an address- and day-level map of urban stressors (Summary).
- They "capture what residents actually experience and perceive, which objective monitoring often misses" (Summary).
- Scalable to "any city with a 311 system"; adds layers to the pending NIA R01 on the urban exposome ([[r01-urban-exposome-aging]]) (Summary).

## Design at a glance
- Aim 1 (chronic): yearly noise, housing-condition (e.g., loss of heat) and rodent complaints vs incident MI, stroke and heart failure in adult Mount Sinai patients.
- Aim 2 (acute): daily complaint spikes near home vs cardiovascular ED visits and admissions, case-crossover.
- Team: Knobel (PI), Yitshak-Sade, Kloog, Midya. Cores: BDSFC (Gennings), CEC (Galvez).

## Concepts used

- [[environmental-noise]] — noise complaints as one stressor.
