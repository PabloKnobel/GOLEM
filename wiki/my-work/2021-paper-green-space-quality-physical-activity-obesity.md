**Lead.** Pablo's first-author cross-sectional study linking RECITAL audits of 149 Barcelona green spaces to physical activity, overweight/obesity and green space use in 2,053 adults; available online December 29, 2020 (*Environmental Pollution* 271, 2021); corresponding author R. Maneja ([[knobel2021quality]], p. 1). Evidence summary: [[knobel2021quality]].

## How I framed it
- Inconsistency explained by quality: "One possible explanation for this inconsistency could be that these studies have not taken account of the quality characteristics of green spaces" (p. 2).
- Claimed novelty: "the first epidemiological study to simultaneously evaluate and compare the association of different UGS quality dimensions" with these outcomes (p. 5).
- Mean over maximum: "instead of having a single very high quality UGS, it might be more beneficial to improve the quality of the majority, if not all, of UGS" (p. 6).
- Birds: bird biodiversity "is overlooked in most UGS quality assessments for epidemiologic studies" (p. 6).
- Access: "access to UGS depends on more factors than proximity per se" (p. 6).
