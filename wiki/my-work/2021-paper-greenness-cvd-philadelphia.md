**Lead.** Pablo's first-author ecological study of greenness measures and cardiovascular risk factors across Philadelphia census tracts, published online March 22, 2021 in *Environmental Research*; he led conceptualization, methodology, formal analysis and the original draft ([[knobel2021associations]], p. 1, 8). Evidence summary: [[knobel2021associations]].

## How I framed it
- Terminology: "greenness" for any vegetated area and "green space" for the structured subset, a distinction that "adds a layer of quality to the greenness concept" (p. 2).
- Measures matched to pathways: presence alone may matter for air quality, whereas for physical activity greenness "must be perceived to be useable, accessible, inclusive, and/or safe" (p. 2).
- "Perceived and objective metrics can, and should, be used as complementary measures" (p. 2).
- Equity framing: race and SES "may modify associations between greenness and cardiovascular disease in different ways" (p. 2); results read against the equigenic hypothesis and structural racism (p. 7).
- Policy line: "Choosing the best option between increasing the amount of green or facilitating access to the existing one might depend on the sociodemographic context" (p. 8).
