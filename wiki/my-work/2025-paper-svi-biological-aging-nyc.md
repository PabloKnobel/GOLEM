**Lead.** Pablo's first- and corresponding-author EHR study of census-tract social vulnerability and phenotypic age acceleration in older New Yorkers, published online January 14, 2025 ([[knobel2025social]], p. 240). Evidence summary: [[knobel2025social]].

## How I framed it
- Heterogeneity of aging: "The heterogeneity of age-related decline is not random, as personal and environmental exposures can promote or impede healthy aging" (p. 241).
- Gap in the Social Hallmarks: "Originally, the Social Hallmarks of Aging left out the broader socioeconomic context" (p. 241).
- Social exposome: "it is critical to tackle social environment exposures from a social exposome perspective" (p. 241).
- EHR rationale: common clinical biomarkers make PhenoAge usable "in large retrospective cohorts using EHR" (p. 241).
- Mixture argument: an overall SVI score "might oversimplify" the links; the mixture approach evaluates themes "simultaneously, as they occur in real life" (p. 245).
- Geroscience close: biological aging as "a modifiable process, therefore targetable with policy and action" (p. 246–247).
