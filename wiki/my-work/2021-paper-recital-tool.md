**Lead.** Pablo's first-author paper presenting RECITAL, a 90-item in-situ green space quality audit applied to 149 Barcelona green spaces; available online October 24, 2020 (volume 57, 2021). He is credited with conceptualization, methodology, data curation, the original draft and project administration; Dadvand is corresponding ([[knobel2021development]], p. 1, 7). Evidence summary: [[knobel2021development]].

## How I framed it
- Gap: most studies "have relied on the quantity or presence of green spaces, not considering the potentially important effect of the quality of these spaces" (p. 1).
- Quality definition (after Gidlow et al. 2018): "the attributes that affect the use and interaction of the population with the UGS" (p. 1).
- Inclusiveness: RECITAL "aims to encompass all potentially relevant quality aspects … both if they are well known or have little supporting evidence" (p. 2).
- Novelty: "the most comprehensive tool in terms of the number of quality dimensions included" and "the first tool to systematize the definition and selection of UGS" (p. 5).
- Modularity: users can "explore novel potential pathways, focus on specific quality aspects … or limit their scope to a few number of items" (p. 7).
