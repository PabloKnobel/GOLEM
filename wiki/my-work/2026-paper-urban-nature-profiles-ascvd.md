## How I framed it
- Problem: "most exposure metrics oversimplify complex environmental conditions, limiting their relevance for public health and equity" (p. 1).
- Beyond NDVI: "It is not only the mere presence of vegetation that matters, but also key qualities such as accessibility, usability, vegetation diversity, and the presence of water bodies" (p. 1).
- Exposome link: examining multiple dimensions simultaneously is "an idea central to the exposome framework" (p. 2).
- Interpretation: "increased nature cover, while potentially relevant, might not be the main driver of the urban nature benefits on cardiovascular health" (p. 4).
- Policy: "Equitable urban design should consider multiple forms of accessible, biodiverse nature to maximize health benefits across diverse communities" (p. 6).
