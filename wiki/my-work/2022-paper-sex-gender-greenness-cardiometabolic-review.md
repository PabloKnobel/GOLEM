## How I framed it
- Gap: "the examination of the contribution of sex and gender regarding the benefits of urban greenness for people's cardiometabolic health is lacking" (p. 1054).
- Call: "research needs to use the proper terminology for sex and gender and policy makers should design urban greenness with a gender perspective" (p. 1054).
- Quality gap: "None of the articles assessed the quality of greenness" (p. 1057); "a substantial gap" (p. 1062).
- Policy: "Planning for green space should integrate a gender perspective" (p. 1063); "Only through care and justice-centred policies ... can a green city be a just city for all" (p. 1064).
- Note: Pablo is senior, not first, author; wording reflects the author team.
