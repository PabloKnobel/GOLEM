Evidence summary: [[knobel2023socioeconomic]].

## How I framed it
- Gap: studies focus on PM2.5 mass, but "which specific sources drive neighborhood disparities in PM2.5 and its components exposure levels remain unclear" (p. 2).
- Rationale: health effects of each component and source "can be unique and independent from each other" (p. 2).
- Structural framing: redlining as "a form of structural racism" and highway siting that "purposely targeted black communities" (p. 2).
- SES measured on several dimensions: "our study highlights that these associations are divergent for different SES indicators" (p. 5).
- Policy line: findings "can guide policymakers in creating policies targeted not only to the general reduction of air pollution exposure but also to ameliorate the situation of the most vulnerable population groups" (p. 7).
