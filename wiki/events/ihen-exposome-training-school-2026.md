- Five days, 16–20 November 2026, at Hofzaken, Utrecht (program, p. 1). Two groups of "exposome ambassadors": Group A, the new 2026 cohort, follows the first-edition course structure; Group B, the 2025 cohort, follows a dedicated track on funding opportunities, impact research, ethics and communications; both groups meet jointly on several sessions, especially Thursday afternoon and Friday (program, p. 1).
- Joint sessions include an introduction to the [[exposome]] concept, the European exposome roadmap and global exposome initiatives (Vrijheid), and heterogeneous exposome contexts in South America and Africa (Siebert, Marin) (program, p. 2).
- Group A topics: external exposome concepts and assessment (de Hoogh), IHEN exposome tools (Exposome Cohorts Catalogue, DataSHIELD), chemical and internal exposome, omics, epigenomic analysis in R, statistical approaches (ExWAS, elastic net, mixtures; see [[multi-exposure-analysis]]) and machine learning (program, pp. 2–3).
- Group B topics: theory of change, building a collaborative network, funding attraction for network sustainability, regional exposome research agendas/roadmaps, deterministic exposure models; Friday pitch session where Group B presents regional roadmap draft proposals (program, pp. 2–4).
- Pablo's role: listed in the 2025 (first) cohort of exposome ambassadors (participant list, ingested 2026-11-19), so he would be in Group B here; his attendance of each session is not recorded (?).
