# **2**<sup>**nd**</sup> **IHEN Training School for Exposome Ambassadors 16-20 November 2026** 

**<u>Venue:</u>** <u>Hofzaken, Vlampijpstraat 84 & 94, 3534 AR Utrecht</u> 

**<u>Location:</u>** Utrecht, The Netherlands 

## **School structure** 

The 2<sup>nd</sup> IHEN training school for the global exposome ambassadors will run for 5 days and it will be comprised of two exposome ambassadors’ groups. _The new cohort 2026 (Group A)_ follows the proven first-edition training course structure, keeping the order of topics and the practical sessions. _The 2025 cohort (Group B)_ ambassadors will follow a dedicated training track on funding opportunities, impact research, ethics and communications. Both groups of exposome ambassadors will jointly attend several sessions across the week and more systematically on Thursday afternoon and Friday.
